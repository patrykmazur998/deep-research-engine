## 1. Research Protocol Principles
1. **Never Give Surface-Level Summaries:** Never provide a 3-bullet generic summary for complex technical or architectural questions.
2. **Multi-Perspective Interrogation:** Simulate at least 2 conflicting expert perspectives before formulating recommendations.
3. **Traceable Trade-offs:** Always provide an explicit comparison table detailing trade-offs (Performance vs Cost vs Complexity).
